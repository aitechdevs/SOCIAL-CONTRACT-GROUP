# WhatsApp display and heading colours

Upload `scg-brand-display-update.zip` to the existing `socialcontractgroup.org` document root and extract all files, replacing matching files. Back up index.php and assets outside the web root first. Clear hosting/CDN cache and hard refresh. The ZIP preserves backend files, database settings, routing and form notifications.

The WhatsApp SVG now has explicit width and height attributes as a fallback. A new last-loaded, content-hashed shared stylesheet bounds its button and icon independently of older stylesheet versions. Mobile positioning keeps it clear of slider controls.

Logo colours sampled from the artwork: forest green #034937 and gold #c09240. Page and section headings use forest green; slider and footer headings use gold against dark backgrounds. Navigation uses logo green, and the navigation button retains white text for contrast. Other typography and layouts are retained.

Office contacts are grouped into labelled Email, Phone and WhatsApp rows with separate clickable email lines, mobile wrapping and a restrained branded panel.
