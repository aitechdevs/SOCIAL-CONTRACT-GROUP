# Mobile slider fit

Upload and extract `scg-mobile-slider-update.zip` in the existing socialcontractgroup.org document root, replacing matching files after a backup of index.php and assets. Extract the complete assets folder; clear hosting/CDN cache and hard refresh.

At widths up to 900px, slides retain their complete landscape frame at screen width. Mobile zoom is disabled so it cannot crop the edges. Captions appear in a separate dark green panel beneath the image; controls sit below captions. The mobile topic row still scrolls horizontally but its visible scrollbar is hidden. Desktop slideshow design is retained. No configuration, database, backend or routing files are included.
